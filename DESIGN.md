# Sorted design system

Direction: calm, trustworthy and modern. A premium back office for U.S. small business owners. White and light sections alternate with deep navy bands, and one accent color per service keeps pages lively without feeling busy.

## Color

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| ink | #0F1E33 | Headings, strong text, navy bands, footer |
| blue | #1656E0 | Primary buttons, links, closing call band |
| blue-d | #0F3FAE | Hover and pressed state of blue |
| mist | #EEF2F8 | Alternating light sections |
| slate | #566275 | Body and secondary text |
| line | #DCE2EB | Borders and dividers |
| white | #FFFFFF | Page background, cards |

Headline highlight: a blue to teal gradient (#1656E0 to #14A58A on white, #6E9BFF to #4FD1B8 on navy) on the second half of a headline only.

## Typography

- **Headlines and UI:** Heebo 800 for h1 and h2 (letter spacing -0.03em), 700 for card titles, 400 to 600 for text. Supports Hebrew and Latin.
- **Accent serif:** Frank Ruhl Libre 500, used only for the homepage hero, the statement line and FAQ questions on the homepage.
- **Small labels:** 0.8rem, 600, uppercase, 0.14em tracking, with a short line before them.
- No trailing periods on headlines (the slogan "Your U.S. business. Handled." is the exception).

## Layout and spacing

- Content width 1200px, 32px side padding (20px on phones).
- Sections 120 to 128px tall on desktop, 80px on tablet and phone.
- Service pages follow one pattern: hero with an illustration card, navy "what's included" grid, "Is this right for you" with a blue "What you receive" box, 4 numbered steps, 4 questions, blue closing call.

## Shape and depth

- Radius: 22 to 28px for cards and panels, full pill for buttons and chips, 12px for form fields.
- Cards on white: 1px line border and a soft shadow, lifting 3 to 4px on hover.
- Cards on navy: 4.5% white surface, 9% white border, a colored 3px line on top.

## Components

- **Primary button:** blue pill, white text, WhatsApp icon. On blue bands it turns white with ink text.
- **Text link:** ink with a 1.5px underline, blue on hover.
- **Why Work With Sorted box:** navy panel with a blue glow and colored check rows.
- **Language:** English is the default. Hebrew switches the whole page to right to left; arrows and offsets flip.

## Imagery

- Real, calm photos of owners at work only. No staged handshakes, suits or skylines.
- Illustration cards (search ad, formation package, task lists) are labeled as illustrations where they could be mistaken for real results.
