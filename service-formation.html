What's Included

Every Step, Clearly Explained

Business Formation (LLC, S Corp, or C Corp)

We register your business with the state and advise you on choosing the right structure. A Limited Liability Company (LLC) is straightforward and flexible, an S Corp can lower your self-employment taxes once you're profitable, and a C Corp is built for bringing in investors. Whichever you choose, your business becomes a separate legal entity that protects your personal assets and positions you as an established business with banks, clients, partners, and investors.

Registered Agent

Every state requires you to name a Registered Agent before registering your business. They are a person or company with a physical address in that state that officially receives legal and government mail on your behalf. This also keeps your residential address off public records. We arrange this service for you if needed.

DBA Filing

A DBA ("Doing Business As") allows you to operate and market your business under a name other than its legal registered name. Depending on your location, it is filed with the state or the county. Without a DBA, banks and clients recognize only your legal business name, and payments issued to your brand name may be declined.

EIN (Federal Tax ID)

An Employer Identification Number (EIN) is the federal tax identification number for your business. It is required to open a business bank account, hire employees, register for state taxes, receive payments, and file taxes correctly. The IRS issues it at no cost, and we manage the application process, even if you do not have a Social Security Number (SSN).

Operating Agreement

An Operating Agreement is an internal document that defines the ownership of your business and how decisions are made. Most banks require an Operating Agreement to open a business account. In the event of a dispute, a disagreement between partners, or an audit, it protects your interests and serves as formal proof that your business is legitimate.

Organized Business Document Package

Upon completion, you receive all of your formation documents, including your EIN confirmation letter, filed Articles of Organization, and Operating Agreement, organized, clearly labeled, and explained in plain language. As an added benefit of using Sorted's services, you will receive a free downloadable checklist of next steps, such as registering for state taxes and meeting your annual state requirements, together with additional resources to help you plan your next moves.

Good to Know

Read all questions

How long does it take?

Typically up to two weeks depending on the state, with faster expedited options available for an additional fee.

Do I need to live in the U.S. to open a business there?

Not necessarily. Many owners form a U.S. LLC while living abroad. We'll walk you through what applies to your situation.

Do I need an EIN, and can I get one without a Social Security number?

In most cases, yes. An EIN is needed to open a business bank account, hire employees, and file taxes. You don't need a Social Security number (SSN) to get one. Without an SSN, the application can't be completed online, so it usually takes a little longer and an additional $50 service fee applies. We prepare and file the application for you as part of our business formation service.

Are state fees included in your prices?

Our prices are for our service. Government and state filing fees are separate, and we'll tell you the exact costs in advance, before anything is filed.
